# Task Tracker+ Privacy Policy

**Effective:** November 4, 2026

Task Tracker+ is a project and task tracker that runs inside Root communities. It is an independent app made by CJXanderr ("we", "us"). It is not made by, affiliated with or endorsed by Root Communications, Inc. ("Root").

This policy explains what data Task Tracker+ handles, how it is used and shared, and how you can have it changed or deleted. It covers only Task Tracker+. Your use of Root itself is covered by [Root's Privacy Policy](https://www.rootapp.com/privacy-policy) and [Terms of Use](https://www.rootapp.com/terms-of-use). If anything here conflicts with Root's terms, Root's terms apply.

## Who handles your data

Task Tracker+ runs entirely on Root's platform. Root hosts the app: each community that installs it gets its own app server and database, run by Root, and attached files are kept in Root's file storage.

Under [Root's Developer Terms](https://www.rootapp.com/developer-terms-of-service), Root is the controller of the personal data in the app's data. We process it on Root's behalf, only to run Task Tracker+.

## What the app collects

When you use Task Tracker+, it stores:

- **Your Root user ID.** It is saved with the things you create or change, so the app can show who did what. Your display name and profile picture are read from Root when they are shown; the app does not store them.
- **What you add.** Projects (name, description, short identifier and image), tasks (title, description, phase, priority, assignee, tags and due date), comments, tags, and the projects you mark as favorites.
- **Files you attach.** The files themselves are uploaded to Root's file storage. The app keeps a record of each file with its name, type, size, who uploaded it and when.
- **Task history.** Who created a task, who changed its phase, priority, assignee, tags or due date, who added or removed its files, and when.
- **Access settings.** Who can view, contribute to or manage each project (members and roles, kept as Root member groups), and the members and roles your community's admins choose as Task Tracker Admins and Project Creators in the app's settings.
- **Technical logs.** Error messages written by the app's server, which can include Root IDs. Root keeps these logs, and we can see them in Root's Developer Portal.

The app does not collect your email address, password, payment details, location, contacts or device information. It uses no cookies, analytics, tracking or ads, and it does not send data to any service outside Root.

## How the data is used

Only to run Task Tracker+: showing projects, tasks, comments and files to the people who have access, keeping each task's history, applying project permissions, and sending notifications.

When you are assigned to a task or taken off one, or when a task you created or are assigned to gets a comment or moves to another phase, the app asks Root to send a notification to the people involved. It contains the task's key and name, the name of the person who made the change and, for comments, the start of the comment.

We never use this data for advertising or marketing, to build profiles of you, or to train AI models, and we never sell it.

## Who can see it

- **People in your community** who can view a project can see its tasks, comments, files and history, and can download its files. Task Tracker Admins can see every project. Your community's admins and each project's managers decide who has access.
- **Root,** which hosts the app and its data under Root's own terms and privacy policy.
- **Us,** only through the technical logs described above. We do not access your community's projects, tasks, comments or files.

We do not share the data with anyone else, except when the law requires it.

## How long it is kept

Data is kept until it is deleted in the app, or until the community uninstalls Task Tracker+.

- Deleting a comment deletes the files on it. Deleting a task deletes its comments, history and file records. Deleting a project deletes all of its tasks, comments, history, file records and access settings.
- Files that were attached to an unfinished comment or new task, and never posted, are removed from the app after 24 hours.
- When a community uninstalls Task Tracker+, Root shuts down that community's app server and database. We keep no copies.
- Root's platform currently gives apps no way to erase a stored file. When a file is removed in the app, the app deletes its record and the file no longer appears in Task Tracker+, but the stored copy stays in Root's file storage under Root's policies. To have it erased, contact Root (see below).
- People who downloaded a file keep their own copy, which neither we nor Root can delete.

## Changing or deleting your data

- **In the app:** you can edit or delete your own comments and the files on them. If you can contribute to a project, you can edit or delete the tasks you created and remove the files you uploaded to tasks. A project's managers can edit or delete any task in it, and delete the project.
- **Ask your community:** for other content, ask the project's managers or your community's admins.
- **Ask us:** open an issue at [github.com/CJXanderr/TaskTrackerPlus/issues](https://github.com/CJXanderr/TaskTrackerPlus/issues), saying which community and what you want changed or deleted. We will respond promptly and, with your community's admins or Root, make sure it is done. Issues on GitHub are public, so do not include personal details there. If you would rather not post publicly, use Root's channels below.
- **Ask Root:** Root is the controller of this data. To use your privacy rights, such as access, correction or deletion, contact Root through its [support form](https://www.rootapp.com/support) or at support@rootapp.com with the subject "International Privacy Inquiry". You can delete your Root account at [rootapp.com/delete-account](https://www.rootapp.com/delete-account). Privacy requests sent to us are forwarded to Root, as Root's Developer Terms require.

## Security

The data stays inside Root's hosted platform, and project permissions control who can see it. If we learn of unauthorized access to the app's data, we will tell Root right away and notify the affected users where the law requires it.

## Children

Task Tracker+ is not meant for children. Root does not allow anyone under 16 to use its service, and Root users who are 16 or 17 need a parent or guardian's agreement under Root's Terms of Use.

## Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page and update the date at the top. If a change affects what data the app collects or how it is used, we will also say so in the app's release notes.
